# P6 follow-up novelty: tuning result / P6 补查新颖性：调参结果 (INCOMPLETE / 未完成)

- EN: Frozen criteria `../P6-FOLLOWUP-NOVELTY-CRITERIA.md`. Tuning pool 649, baseline N4200 = 446 complete raw at 8,236 B. Byte-matched controls (more turns, no model call): R1 489 (+43/0, 9,881 B), R2 504, R3 521, R4 534, R5 537 (+91/0, 15,764 B). Follow-ups with no novelty limit (1 planner call/question, 3 retrievals): A-F0 481 (+35/0, 9,801 B) loses to the same-size control (28 gained / 36 lost, p = 0.38). Novelty-limited arms gain +7 to +23 at +130 to +670 B but none beats its matched rung (frozen rule: no eligible arm, pick = none). Under the frozen rule the final set was NOT run and the verdict is **failed**.
- 中文：冻结标准见 `../P6-FOLLOWUP-NOVELTY-CRITERIA.md`。调参池 649 题，基线 N4200 原文完整 446（8,236 B）。字节匹配对照（多放轮次，无模型调用）：R1 489（+43/0，9,881 B）……R5 537（+91/0，15,764 B）。无新颖性限制的补查（每题 1 次规划调用、3 次检索）：A-F0 481（+35/0，9,801 B），不敌同体积对照（增 28 / 减 36，p = 0.38）。带新颖性限制的组在 +130 至 +670 B 下增 +7 至 +23，但无一击败其匹配档位（冻结规则：无合格组，无入选）。按冻结规则**未运行**最终集，结论为**失败**。
- EN: Caveat: the frozen ladder has no rung below +1.6 KB, so tiny-byte arms were compared with a much larger control. Post-hoc, exploratory (not frozen, tuning only): finer controls X17/X18/X20/X22 (MAX 17-22) show follow-ups roughly tie them at equal bytes (e.g. A-MM 467 vs X18 462 at 8,539 vs 8,800 B, 21 gained / 16 lost, p = 0.51; A-OV vs X18 18/16, p = 0.86; A-F0 vs X22 28/36). Follow-ups find real new gold turns (A: 97 of 537 follow-ups useful, 294 clearly useless, mean overlap 0.58; B: 31 of 171 useful, 69 useless) but not more per byte than simply delivering a few more first-call turns, and they cost a model call. Novelty limits cut waste (A-F0 1,308 B admitted → A-OV 335 B for 18 of 35 gains) without beating the control.
- 中文：注意：冻结的档位阶梯在 +1.6 KB 以下没有档位，字节很少的组被拿去和大得多的对照比较。事后、探索性（未冻结，仅调参池）：更细的对照 X17/X18/X20/X22（MAX 17–22）显示，同等字节下补查与之大致持平（如 A-MM 467 对 X18 462，8,539 对 8,800 B，增 21 / 减 16，p = 0.51；A-OV 对 X18 为 18/16，p = 0.86；A-F0 对 X22 为 28/36）。补查确能找到真正新的金标轮次（A：537 次补查中 97 次有用、294 次明显无用，平均重叠 0.58；B：171 次中 31 次有用、69 次无用），但每字节并不比首次调用多给几轮更好，还要多一次模型调用。新颖性限制减少了浪费（A-F0 准入 1,308 B → A-OV 335 B，保留 35 个增益中的 18 个），但仍未超过对照。
